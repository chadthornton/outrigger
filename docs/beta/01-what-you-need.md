# What Outrigger is, and what you need

Outrigger is a small touchscreen board that sits on a USB hub next to your FM-1. It lets a MIDI controller with a USB
connection play the FM-1 with no computer, and it switches the FM-1's firmware for you, backing it up first.

**The homepage has the full shopping list, with links: [outrigger.music](https://outrigger.music/#need).** In short:

- **An ESP32-S3 board with a screen.** Tested: Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.69.
- **A powered USB-C hub** that can charge a laptop through it. Outrigger plugs in where the laptop would.
- **Your FM-1.**
- **A MIDI controller with a USB connection.** Tested: Novation Launchkey Mini MK4 37.
- **To install:** Chrome or Edge on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer, and a USB-C data cable.

## The fine print

Outrigger is an unofficial, independent project. It isn't made or supported by M-VAVE or Waveshare. It's a beta:
use it at your own risk, and read [If your FM-1 won't boot](06-if-your-fm1-wont-boot.md) before your first firmware
switch.
