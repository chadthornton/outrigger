Help

What's an ESP32?

A small, cheap computer on a chip, made by Espressif and found in lots of maker gadgets. Outrigger runs on one that comes with a 1.69-inch touchscreen and a USB-C port.

My keyboard has a 5-pin or 3.5 mm MIDI out

You don't need Outrigger to play: a cable into the FM-1's MIDI IN does it. You'd still want it for switching firmware.

Update Outrigger on my board

On the board: Settings, then tap Computer twice. Plug it into this computer and click Install. Your backups stay.

No port shows up

Try another cable: charge-only cables never show up. Or hold BOOT, tap RST, let go of BOOT, and click Install again.

My FM-1 won't start

It's almost certainly not dead. The Firmware Hub's Rescue, the Unbricker or the Transporter can bring it back from a computer.
