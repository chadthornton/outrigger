What you need

An ESP32-S3 board with a screen

Works today: Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.69, a 240×280 touchscreen. Buy on Amazon ↗

Next, we expect: Waveshare's 1.8-inch AMOLED and 3.5-inch touch boards, once Outrigger supports them.

It has to be an ESP32-S3 with native USB. Skip boards whose only USB port says "one-click download": they have a converter chip and can't host a keyboard.

A powered USB-C hub

  • Its own power input: a USB-C charger port, or a barrel jack.
  • Pass-through charging to its host port, the one a laptop would use. Outrigger plugs in there and runs on that power.
  • USB-C data ports for the FM-1 and your controller. On the hub we tested, only the USB-C ports worked.
  • USB 2.0 is plenty.

Before you buy

What's an ESP32?

A small, cheap computer on a chip, found in lots of maker gadgets. Outrigger runs on one that comes with a touchscreen and a USB-C port, so it needs no soldering.

What's an FM-1?

A pocket FM synthesizer from M-VAVE, with 27 keys, a speaker and a battery. Players write their own firmware for it, which is what Outrigger switches between.

What does "firmware" mean here?

The software the FM-1 runs. Swap it and the same hardware becomes a different instrument: a groovebox, a drum machine, a chord instrument.

My keyboard has a 5-pin or 3.5 mm MIDI out

Then a MIDI cable into the FM-1's MIDI IN plays it, no Outrigger needed. You'd still want Outrigger to switch firmware.

Which controllers work?

USB MIDI keyboards and pads that work without a driver, which is most of them. One at a time: the board has room for the hub, the FM-1 and one controller.

Troubleshooting

Update Outrigger on my board

On the board, open Settings and tap Computer twice. Plug it into your computer and click Install. Your backups stay.

Install can't find the board

Try another cable: charge-only cables never show up. Or hold BOOT, tap RST, let go of BOOT, and click Install again.

My FM-1 won't start

It's almost certainly not dead: its install mode lives in ROM. The Firmware Hub's Rescue or the Unbricker brings it back from a computer.

A second keyboard shows up but doesn't play

The board has room for one controller next to the FM-1. Unplug the other one.

Where do my backups live?

On the board, never online. Copying them to a computer comes in a later version.
