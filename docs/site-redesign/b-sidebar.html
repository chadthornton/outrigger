Play from any USB keyboard

One cable to the FM-1, one to your controller. Power and MIDI travel together.

Instead of what?

Without it, a USB-only controller needs a computer, or a MIDI box with TRS and 5-pin DIN adapters. If your keyboard already has a MIDI out, a cable does the job.

⇄

Switch firmware, keep every song

Load any of 100+ firmwares right on the board. It backs up first, and your songs return when you switch back.

Beta: needs one setup from a Mac first.

How is it safe?

It reads the FM-1 twice before writing, never touches its boot area, and checks every byte it writes. The FM-1 can always get back into install mode.

Install in two minutes

  1. Plug the board into this computer.
  2. Click Install and pick the port it offers.
  3. Move the board to the hub and play.
Leave “Erase device” unticked: it deletes your backups.

Chrome or Edge on a computer.

What's an ESP32?

A small, cheap computer on a chip that runs lots of maker gadgets. This board adds a 1.69-inch touchscreen and a USB-C port.

Help

Update my board

Settings, then tap Computer twice. Plug in and click Install. Backups stay.

My FM-1 won't start

It's almost certainly not dead. Try the Firmware Hub's Rescue, the Unbricker or the Transporter.

No port shows up

Use a data cable, not a charge-only one. Or hold BOOT, tap RST, release BOOT, and try again.

Where do backups live?

On the board, never online. Copying them to a computer comes later.
